# Learn a subject one lesson at a time

Nous Reader turns your study materials or a topic into a course you can read, listen to and ask questions about. Lessons build on one another so you can work through a subject with the explanation and study tools together.

![A narrated probability lesson in Nous: the spoken passage appears beside a formula, with its current symbol highlighted and playback controls below.](../../docs/assets/lesson-playback.png)

The lesson player follows the narration and highlights the symbol being explained. This example lesson is in Italian.

## Try a course

[Create your first course](01-section-overview/01-p-overview.md), then [study a lesson](05-section-frontend/01-p-reader-shell.md). You can start from your own documents or choose a topic.
