You are studying the Linux course and want to understand a visual explanation alongside its text. Sign in, use the course outline to open the first lesson of the second module, which contains a visual explanation, and inspect that visual and the surrounding explanation. Use the visual preview or enlargement control when available, then return to the lesson. Judge whether the labels are readable and whether any connections shown correspond to relationships actually explained in the text. Finish after inspecting a rendered visual and returning to readable lesson content; if you cannot find or open one, report that obstacle. Independent items do not need to be connected to each other.
