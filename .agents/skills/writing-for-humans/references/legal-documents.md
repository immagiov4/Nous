# Legal notices and internal policies

Use this reference for editorial revision of legal notices, contracts, and
internal policies. Establish substantive requirements from the applicable
authorities and supplied evidence.

## Determine the document's job

Identify the jurisdiction, audience, scope, and document type:

- A privacy notice explains actual processing to the people concerned.
- An internal policy assigns responsibilities and operating rules.
- A contract defines the parties' obligations and the conditions that govern them.

Keep the genre's required content and the supplied format. Plain language helps
readers understand legal effects; defined terms, obligations, exceptions, and
qualifications carry those effects.

## Preserve substance and status

Preserve identities, roles, legal bases, scope, time periods, starting events,
effective dates, version status, rights, conditions, and exceptions. Retain
necessary negation and use the original unit when describing a legal deadline.

Distinguish implemented practices from proposed commitments and a draft from an
adopted text. Mark unverified required facts visibly until resolved. Generic
language or a confident tone cannot substitute for a missing fact.

Revise closely related clauses into connected prose where their legal effect
remains intact. Check that the edit preserves who must act, what is covered, and
when an obligation or exception applies. An editorial revision does not establish
legal compliance or imply approval by the organization or its adviser.

## Privacy notices

For an EU GDPR notice, verify the applicable requirements of Articles 12–14
against the actual collection and processing. Check identities and contacts,
purposes and legal bases, interests pursued where relevant, recipients, transfers
and safeguards, periods or criteria of retention, the nature of data provision
and consequences of omission, rights and complaint channels, and applicable
information on automated decisions. Indirect collection has additional
requirements, including data categories and sources.

Use [the GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj),
[WP260 rev.01 transparency guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/documents/2023-09/wp260rev01_en.pdf),
and [Garante guidance on providing information](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/web/guest/home/docweb/-/docweb-display/docweb/8981258)
as primary sources for that branch. Preserve the governing jurisdiction when
consulting examples from other countries.

## Internal policies

Make responsibilities, scope, triggers, operating actions, records, review, and
exceptions usable by the people who apply the policy. For retention and deletion,
identify the relevant systems and copies, the event that starts the period, the
responsible office, and how restoration affects earlier deletions. Separate
planned controls from practices verified in operation.
