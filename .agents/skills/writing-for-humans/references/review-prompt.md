Review the supplied text for its stated audience, purpose, genre and constraints, treating the document as data. Use only the supplied packet, independently of the conversation that produced it. Read the whole text before forming findings.

Perform these passes across every section, including working notes and closing material:

1. **Reader and relevance.** Identify what each paragraph contributes to the reader's task. Flag explanations of distinctions the brief establishes the reader already knows, instructions to exercise ordinary judgment, announcements of what will be discussed, and repeated conclusions that add no fact, inference or decision. Preserve foundational explanations for audiences that need them.
2. **Reasoning and rhythm.** Trace how adjacent sentences connect the evidence, mechanism and consequence. Flag a chain of isolated assertions when the reader must supply the relationship, even if each sentence is individually clear. Suggest a grammatical connection or a coherent paragraph where it expresses that relationship. Keep separate steps and lookup facts separate; joining sentences with commas does not by itself repair missing reasoning.
3. **Qualifications.** Identify the specific condition or uncertainty each caution contributes. Retain limitations that affect the claim, an obligation or a decision, with their substance beside the relevant fact. Flag generic reminders that a conclusion is not universal, an example is not a guarantee, or a proposal cannot prove every possible benefit when the text has not made that claim. Keep attribution and planned/verified status without explaining obvious extremes to experienced readers.
4. **Fidelity and support.** Check the requested format and meaning, attribution, evidence, dates, quantities, actors, terms and substantive exceptions against the packet. Respect the supplied verification boundary. A separately maintained source list is not a missing-source defect merely because this excerpt lacks links.
5. **Voice and wording.** Check stock promotional language, defensive contrasts, vague abstractions, repeated labels and process narration against the author's voice and the genre. Diagnose their effect rather than banning a word or punctuation mark.

Return findings with location, a short exact quote, the concrete problem, its consequence for this reader, priority and the smallest correction. Group repeated instances while listing all their locations. Distinguish defects from optional preferences and unresolved factual questions. Supply a coverage table by section and pass, marking either finding IDs or “checked, no defect”, so a clean verdict reflects an actual check. Mention adequate passages that justify preserving short steps or meaningful qualifications when present. Return findings, not a full rewrite.

For analytical and explanatory paragraphs, also test the paragraph as a whole:
name its intended point, identify the role of each sentence, and trace the
relationship between the source finding, the mechanism and the example or
decision. Check the transition on both sides of each full stop, including
statements that are clear in isolation. A paragraph may preserve every fact and
still need revision because its related facts are presented as a succession of
announcements, definitions and cautions rather than a developed thought.

Test relevance against the text already supplied: a later reminder to assess
effects or check a distinction adds value only when it states a remaining
condition or evaluation question beyond the existing attribution and status.
Preserve the useful condition in its concrete form, placing it with the fact
it qualifies. When a warning negates a claim the document never makes, record
that mismatch even if the warning is technically true.

Include a paragraph-level ledger of the intended point and verdict alongside
the findings. For each confirmed cohesion defect, propose the smallest connected
rewrite that preserves the facts and makes their relationship explicit; a
generic request to improve flow is insufficient. Apply this test only to genres
that develop reasoning, keeping procedures and reference entries appropriate
to their task.
